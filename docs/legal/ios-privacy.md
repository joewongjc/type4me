# Type4Me for iPhone Privacy Policy / 隐私政策

Last updated: October 25, 2026 · 更新日期：2026 年 10 月 25 日

[English](#english) · [中文](#中文)

## English

Type4Me for iPhone is a voice keyboard. You hold the mic on the keyboard, Type4Me records your speech, sends it to a speech recognition service you choose, and inserts the text. The developer runs no servers for this app and receives none of your data.

### What leaves your iPhone

Type4Me sends data only to the third-party services you set up yourself, with your own account and API key, and only after you agree in the app:

- **Speech recognition service** (for example Volcano Engine, Soniox, Deepgram or OpenAI). It receives the audio recorded while you hold to talk, and your hotwords if you use them.
- **Text model service** (for example DeepSeek, OpenAI, Anthropic Claude or Google Gemini), only when you use a mode that processes text, such as Voice Polish or Translate. It receives the recognized text.

These services handle the data under their own privacy policies and your agreement with them. You can withdraw your permission anytime in Settings › Privacy, which stops dictation until you agree again.

### What stays on your iPhone

- Your dictation history, hotwords, snippets and modes are stored only on this iPhone.
- API keys are stored in the iOS Keychain.
- A diagnostics log of session events (not your dictated text) is kept on this iPhone. It is shared only if you copy it yourself.

### What Type4Me does not do

- It does not log what you type with other keyboards or record anything when you are not holding the mic.
- It does not use analytics, advertising or tracking, and it does not sell or share data with data brokers.
- It has no accounts.

### Update check

To tell you when a newer version is out, Type4Me reads a small public file (`updates-ios.json`) from GitHub. The request carries no personal data, but GitHub sees your IP address, as with any web request.

### Permissions

- **Microphone**: to record your speech while you hold to talk.
- **Camera**: only to scan the QR code shown by Type4Me on your Mac to import your setup.
- **Keyboard Full Access**: lets the keyboard talk to the Type4Me app, which does the recording. iOS requires it for this.

### Deleting your data

Settings › Clear History deletes your history. Settings › Delete Speech, Text Setup & Modes removes your keys and modes. Deleting the app removes everything it stored.

### Children

Type4Me is not directed at children under 13.

### Changes and contact

We will post changes on this page. Questions: open an issue at <https://github.com/joewongjc/type4me/issues>.

## 中文

Type4Me iPhone 版是一个语音键盘：你按住键盘上的麦克风，Type4Me 录下你的话，发给你选择的语音识别服务，再把文字输入到光标处。开发者没有为这个 App 运营任何服务器，也不会收到你的任何数据。

### 哪些数据会离开你的 iPhone

Type4Me 只把数据发给你自己配置的第三方服务（使用你自己的账号和 API Key），并且要先在 App 里征得你的同意：

- **语音识别服务**（例如火山引擎、Soniox、Deepgram、OpenAI）：接收你按住说话期间录下的声音，以及你设置的热词。
- **文本模型服务**（例如 DeepSeek、OpenAI、Anthropic Claude、Google Gemini）：只在你使用需要处理文字的模式（如语音润色、翻译）时，接收识别出的文字。

这些服务按各自的隐私政策和你与它们之间的协议处理数据。你可以随时在「设置 › 隐私」里撤回授权，撤回后重新同意之前不能听写。

### 哪些数据只留在你的 iPhone 上

- 听写历史、热词、片段替换和模式只保存在这台 iPhone 上。
- API Key 保存在 iOS 钥匙串里。
- 诊断记录只记录会话事件（不含你说的内容），保存在本机，只有你自己复制时才会分享出去。

### Type4Me 不做的事

- 不记录你用其他键盘输入的内容，不按住麦克风时不录音。
- 不使用任何统计、广告或追踪，不出售数据，也不与数据经纪商共享。
- 没有账号体系。

### 检查更新

为了在有新版本时提醒你，Type4Me 会从 GitHub 读取一个公开的小文件（`updates-ios.json`）。请求里不带任何个人信息，但和所有网络请求一样，GitHub 能看到你的 IP 地址。

### 权限

- **麦克风**：在你按住说话时录音。
- **相机**：只用于扫描 Mac 版 Type4Me 显示的二维码，导入配置。
- **键盘完全访问**：让键盘和负责录音的 Type4Me App 通信，iOS 要求打开这一项才能做到。

### 删除数据

「设置 › 清空历史」删除历史记录；「设置 › 删除识别、处理配置和模式」删除密钥和模式；删除 App 会清除它保存的全部内容。

### 儿童

Type4Me 不面向 13 岁以下儿童。

### 变更与联系

政策变更会在本页公布。有问题请在 <https://github.com/joewongjc/type4me/issues> 提 issue。
